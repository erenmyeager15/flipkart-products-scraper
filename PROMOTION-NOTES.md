# Flipkart Product Scraper Promotion Notes

## Short positioning

Scrape public Flipkart search result pages for product prices, MRP, discounts, ratings, product IDs, images, and URLs. Best fit: lightweight ecommerce price monitoring, catalog research, and marketplace visibility checks.

## Tutorial video ideas

1. `How to export Flipkart product prices to CSV in 2 minutes`
2. `Track Flipkart prices and discounts with an Apify Actor`
3. `Build a small Flipkart competitor price report without a login`

## 45-second tutorial outline

1. Open the Actor and keep the default `iphone` sample.
2. Set `maxResults` to `1` for the first test.
3. Keep Residential India proxy enabled.
4. Run the Actor and open the dataset.
5. Show price, MRP, discount, rating, image URL, and product URL.
6. Export CSV/Excel or copy the API dataset URL.

## Guardrails

- Do not claim official Flipkart API access.
- Do not claim private seller/customer/account data.
- Do not promise perfect stock availability from search pages.
- Do not encourage spam, resale of restricted data, or terms-violating use.
- Mention that Residential India proxy improves reliability but may add platform usage cost.
